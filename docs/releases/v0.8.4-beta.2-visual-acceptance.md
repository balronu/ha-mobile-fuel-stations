# v0.8.4-beta.2 visual acceptance

The same five mocked stations were rendered through the production bundle at
390 CSS pixels in Chromium. No provider or Home Assistant request was used.

| Mode | Card height | Station/tile heights | Result |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Compact | 444px | 65px × 5 | Reference preserved |
| Detailed | 477px | 68px × 5 | Complete information retained |
| Individual list, normal | 439px | 65px × 5 | Baseline |
| Individual list, compact | 364px | 54px, 54px, 54px, 54px, 52px | Materially denser |
| Individual highlights + list | 346px | 82px, 82px, 54px, 54px, 52px | Two tiles side by side |

The card content width at the 390px viewport is approximately 366px; the two
highlight tiles are approximately 179px wide with an 8px gap. At the tested
narrow container below 359px the tiles stack vertically. Long names remain
ellipsized, prices and navigation remain visible, and the light/dark renders
show no overlap.
